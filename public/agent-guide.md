# Shift Scheduler — Agent Guide

Welcome to the Shift Scheduler! This guide covers everything you need to know as an agent.

---

## Getting Started

### Logging In
- Go to the app URL and sign in with your Google account (@adrevival.io)
- You'll land on the **Schedule** tab showing the weekly view

### Your Profile Settings
Click your name in the top-right corner to access:
- **Timezone** — Set your local timezone so shift times display correctly
- **Country** — Set your country to see your public holidays on the calendar

---

## Understanding Your Schedule

### How Shifts Work
- You are **automatically assigned** to weekday shifts (Monday–Friday) and weekend shifts based on a **rotating schedule**
- Each shift has a **minimum required number of agents** (e.g., 4 or 5). This number may change in the future as the team grows
- Shifts are organized into three types: **EU Shift** (08:00–16:00), **Mid Shift** (00:00–08:00), and **USA Shift** (16:00–00:00)

### Weekend Rotation
- Weekend shifts follow an **alternating rotation**: if you work Saturday this week, you'll work Sunday next week, and vice versa
- This rotation is set up by the admin and repeats automatically for 6 months

### Viewing Your Schedule
- **Schedule tab** — See the full team schedule in Week, Month, or Day view
- **My Shifts tab** — See only your assigned shifts, your days off calendar, and your personal stats

## Flexibility — Swapping & Leaving Shifts

### Swapping Shifts
You can swap a shift with a teammate for flexibility. For example, if you want Monday off but are willing to work an extra day:

1. Go to the **Schedule** tab and click on the shift you want to swap
2. Click **"Swap Shift with Teammate"**
3. Select the teammate you want to swap with
4. Choose which of their shifts you want in return
5. Add an optional reason and send the request
6. Your teammate will get a notification and can accept or decline

### Leaving a Shift (When You're an Extra)
If a shift requires 4 agents and there are 5 assigned (including you), you are the extra agent and can remove yourself:

- On the calendar, click **"Leave"** on the shift card
- On the expanded shift view, click **"Leave Shift"**

> **Important:** You cannot leave a shift if it would drop below the minimum required agents. The system will prevent this.

### Joining a Shift
If you see a shift that needs more agents, you can join it:

- Click **"+ Join"** on any shift card on the calendar
- Or click **"Join Shift"** in the expanded view

## Working Hours

### Your Monthly Target
- Your target is **208 hours per month** (approximately 5 shifts of 8 hours per week, plus weekends)
- You can see your current hours in the **My Shifts** tab header
- Hours turn **red** if you exceed the target (overtime)

### Clocking In & Out
When your shift is about to start, the system will:

1. **Pop up a reminder** to clock in, with a fun GIF and a reminder to greet your teammates in **#support_team_on_duty** on Slack
2. Click **"Let's go!"** to clock in, or **"Snooze 5 min"** to dismiss temporarily

While clocked in:
- A **live timer** appears in the bottom-right corner showing your elapsed time
- The **My Shifts** page shows a green banner with your running clock

When your shift is ending:
- A **pop-up reminder** appears 15 minutes before the end
- It reminds you to say goodbye in **#support_team_on_duty** on Slack
- Click **"Clock Out & Relax"** to clock out

> Your clocked hours count toward your monthly hours total.

## Requesting Time Off

### How to Request
1. Go to **My Shifts** tab
2. Click **"Request Time Off / OOO"**
3. Choose **Full Day** or **Half Day**
4. Select the **date**
5. Choose a **reason**: Vacation, Sick Day, Personal, Family/Emergency, Religious/Public Holiday, or Other
6. Add an optional **note**
7. Click **Submit**

### What Happens Next
- Your request is sent to the admin for **approval**
- You'll see a **⏳ Pending** status on your request
- Once approved or rejected, you'll get a **notification**
- Only **approved** time off counts against your annual allowance

### Your Allowances
- **PTO (Paid Time Off):** 21 days per year (may vary per contract)
- **Sick Days:** 7 days per year
- **Half days** count as 0.5 against your allowance

You can see your remaining balance in the **My Shifts** header (PTO and Sick counters).

### Days Off Calendar
Your **My Shifts** page includes a full calendar showing:
- Your **assigned shifts** as colored bars
- Your **days off** with category labels (Vacation, Sick, etc.)
- Click the **+** on any empty date to request time off
- Use the **X** button on a day off label to cancel it

## Public Holidays

- Your **public holidays** are shown based on the country you set in your profile
- Upcoming holidays appear at the top of the **My Shifts** page
- Holidays also appear on the shared **Schedule** calendar so the whole team can see who has a public holiday
- If you need to take a public holiday off, request it as **"Religious / Public Holiday"** in the time off form

---

## Notifications

Click the **bell icon** in the top-right to see your notifications:
- **Shift assignments** — when you're added to or removed from a shift
- **Swap requests** — when a teammate wants to swap shifts with you
- **Time off updates** — when your time off request is approved or rejected
- **Shift changes** — when a shift you're assigned to is updated or cancelled

## Tips

- 💬 Always say **good morning** and **goodbye** in **#support_team_on_duty** on Slack when starting and ending your shift
- ⏰ Clock in and out consistently — it helps track your hours accurately
- 📅 Request time off **in advance** when possible to give the admin time to ensure coverage
- 🔄 Use the **swap feature** instead of just leaving shifts — it helps maintain coverage
- 👀 Keep an eye on the **filled/required** count on shifts to know if you're needed
